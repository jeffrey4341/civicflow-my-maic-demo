CivicFlow MY Mobile is a working, mobile-first hackathon prototype for multilingual local-council casework in Malaysia. It turns a Malay, English, Chinese or Tamil request into policy-cited, human-owned casework: citizens do not need to know the responsible department, while officers see the next required human action.

The current artifact demonstrates three canonical journeys end to end. A Malay blocked-drain report is classified as drainage and flood risk, grounded in the synthetic Drainage Response SOP, reviewed by an officer, gated for a documented supervisor decision, released to the citizen and closed by a human. A Chinese food-stall licence request stays in the same case while the citizen supplies location, business type and operating hours; the new revision returns to officer review before the reply is released, work is started and the case is closed. An education or welfare request cannot advance through the outcome-sensitive path until a human officer records the welfare outcome; the AI never decides eligibility.

Unlike a generic chatbot, CivicFlow preserves the original request, collects structured follow-up information and keeps consequential actions separate. Saving an officer review does not send a reply. Supervisor approval does not start work. Actionable closure requires a released reply, work in progress and a human closure note; a human-reviewed close-without-action path follows the required supervisor decision. Every recommendation must include selected synthetic policy evidence or remain in manual review, and blocked transitions are recorded in the audit timeline.

The default judging path runs offline through deterministic TypeScript and six synthetic policy documents. All shipped cases, identities, locations and policies are synthetic; no real citizen or government operational data is used. Optional Anthropic assistance, when explicitly configured, may refine detected language, English translation, category and urgency without downgrading deterministic category-specific human gates. The prototype has no production identity, persistent database, government integration or validated multilingual accuracy. "Send reply" and "start work" are demo workflow transitions only.

CivicFlow addresses MAIC T5 - Public Services & Smart Cities through a focused product thesis: multilingual intake, policy evidence, the next human action and a traceable outcome in one governed workflow. The next milestone is not broader feature count. It is to preserve three of three complete canonical journeys with zero unsafe bypasses, package the current product truth for judging and then validate the workflow with one public-service desk using synthetic or formally approved data.
